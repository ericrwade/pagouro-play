Jigsaw by Pagouro: Privacy

It collects nothing. That is the whole policy.

Jigsaw by Pagouro is a free jigsaw puzzle game for Android, made by Pagouro. It has no ads, no tracking and no account.

What the app collects

Nothing. Jigsaw by Pagouro does not collect, send, sell or share any personal or usage data. It does not ask for the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere, and it contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code.

What stays on your phone

  • Your settings (table color, cut, tray, rotation, music and sounds).
  • The puzzle you are working on, so you can close the app and pick up where you left off.

Both are stored only in the app's own storage on your device. Nobody else can see them, including us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

None.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles the download and may keep its own records of it under Google's privacy policy. The app itself adds nothing to that.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes and contact

If this ever changes, this page will say so first, with a new date. Questions: info@pagouro.com.
